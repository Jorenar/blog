{:start="0"}
0. Learn [how to ask questions the smart way](http://www.catb.org/~esr/faqs/smart-questions.html)
1. [`vimtutor`](https://vimhelp.org/usr_01.txt.html#vimtutor) teaches the most basic Vim functionality hands-on
2. [`:h user-manual`](https://vimhelp.org/usr_toc.txt.html) will guide you through every feature from basic to advanced
3. [`:h faq`](https://vimhelp.org/vim_faq.txt.html) - Frequently Asked Questions
4. [`:h quickref`](https://vimhelp.org/quickref.txt.html) - quick reference guide
5. [`:help`](https://vimhelp.org/) and `:helpgrep` to find more details about a specific feature
6. [Check other resources I've gathered](/links#vim)
7. USE IT!
